Lab Formulas & Principles

Scientific Reference Standard

Density Formula

Density ($d$) measures mass per unit volume:

Density (g/mL) = Mass (g) ÷ Volume (mL)

Fluid Displacement (Archimedes' Principle)

When a solid object is submerged underwater, the volume of fluid displaced in milliliters (**mL**) equals the object's total volume ($V$).

Displaced Volume (mL) = Final Level (mL) - Initial Level (200.0 mL)

Floating & Buoyancy

  • An object floats if its density is **less** than the fluid density.
  • Use the "Submerge Floating" button to force floating objects completely underwater and measure their full displacement volume in mL!

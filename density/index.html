Density & Buoyancy Formulas

Scientific Reference Guide

Density Formula

Density ($d$) is mass ($m$) divided by total volume ($V$):

Density (g/mL) = Mass (g) ÷ Volume (mL)

Fluid Displacement Method (Archimedes' Principle)

When a solid object is submerged underwater or in liquid, the volume of fluid displaced equals the volume ($V$) in milliliters (**mL**).

Displaced Volume (mL) = Final Level (mL) - Initial Level (200.0 mL)

Floating Objects & Buoyancy

  • An object floats if its density is **less** than the fluid density (e.g. < 1.00 g/mL in water).
  • When floating naturally, the displaced fluid volume in mL equals the block's **Mass in grams** divided by fluid density.
  • Use the "Submerge Floating" button to force floating objects fully under liquid and measure their total volume (mL)!

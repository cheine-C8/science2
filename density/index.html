Lab Formulas & Principles

Scientific Reference Standard

Density Formula

Density ($d$) measures mass per unit volume:

Density (g/mL) = Mass (g) ÷ Displaced Volume (mL)

Fluid Displacement (Archimedes' Principle)

When an object is fully submerged, the displaced fluid volume ($\Delta V$) equals the total volume of the solid object.

Displaced Volume ($\Delta V$) = Final Level ($V_2$) - Initial Level ($V_1$)

Floating & Buoyancy Mechanics

  • An object floats if its density is less than the fluid's density ($\rho_{object} < \rho_{fluid}$).
  • Use the "Hold Underwater ⬇️" button to push buoyant objects completely underwater to measure their total volume!

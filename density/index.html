Density & Archimedes Principle

Essential Science Concepts

Density Formula

Density ($d$) is defined as mass ($m$) per unit volume ($V$):

Density (g/cm³) = Mass (g) ÷ Volume (cm³)

Water Displacement Method

When an object is submerged in water, the volume of water displaced is exactly equal to the volume ($V$) of the object!

Object Volume = Final Water Level - Initial Water Level (200 mL)

Floating Objects (Buoyancy)

  • Objects with density < 1.0 g/cm³ float in water.
  • When floating naturally, a block displaces water equal to its Mass.
  • To find its true total Volume, use the "Submerge Floating" button to push it completely underwater!

House rules

How to play (hot seat)

  1. The guesser from the active team sits in the hot seat with their back to the screen.
  2. Their teammates can see the card. They give clues so the guesser says the word on top. They can't say the word itself, any of the forbidden words, or parts of them ("pumpkin" can't be "pump").
  3. Everyone else also sees the card and listens for forbidden words. If they hear one, they shout "BOO!" and the teacher presses Taboo: −1 point.
  4. No gestures, sounds-like or "rhymes with" clues, and no spelling. A new guesser takes the hot seat every turn.
